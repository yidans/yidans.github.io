# Public Scholarship

## [Global Trafficking Intelligence Hub](https://globaltrafficking.org)

**Founder and Lead Developer** *(2024-Present)*

I built and maintain this public knowledge hub with the USC Artificial Intelligence and Complex Systems group and support from the Kroner Family Foundation. It curates reports, country- and region-level assessments, legal frameworks, datasets, and academic research across eight world regions, with a searchable interface for practitioners, policymakers, and researchers.
